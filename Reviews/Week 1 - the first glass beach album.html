the first glass beach album

Week 1 of Weekly Reviews is here! Track 1

classic j dies and goes to hell part 1

I think one of the most important things in any Album is the opening few seconds of the first Track (or skit) because that is the single biggest expectation setter from within the work itself, every song has it's identity but the first Song is what pushes the Album's identity and I think classic j nails it on the fucking head because by God the only thing I can do whenever I hear this song is close my eyes and imagine the setting of the Album artwork. The opening verse is something I shoudn't go super in depth into the relatability of because I feel like the whole point is that almost anyone can relate to the idea of, particularly the whole "Staring at the ceiling wide awake Reliving all my worst mistakes" deal but for me personally (which is why I'm writing here in the first place it's about me and I'm selfish and self centred and you should give me attention) there's something about "'Til I finally got a decent job And I got a place I'll never own" the resonates with me on such a strong metaphysical level that I just feel proud everytime I hear it, a little "Fuck you" to everyone in my life who that thought I never COULD have a job (Though as many people as that is, the stress of it all sometimes makes me wish they were right ('– ⌓ – )) and then I would be remiss to not mention the fact that I can perfectly match the pitch of J screaming out "To make it feel more like a home"

Then there's the shift, the bass becomes more of the focus and the vocals have a soft muffle to them really selling the water atmosphere "Tryna keep my head above the waves" there to make that particularly obvious if you're a little too slow (it's okay I still love you <3) followed by the all time most obvious transgender line in all of music "I'm making friends who don't know who I am". I'd be lying if I said it didn't feel good knowing that nobody knows who I used to be. Shoving in my personal life even more the Fridays nights, Foggy streets and Christmas lights that J speaks of so eerily flood me with the memories I made walking around the Canadian suburbs over that Christmas while sharing a Joint. In Australia I don't see fog, that's such a core part of how I view Canada and it's probably a little stupid to see so much of myself in any work as personal as the things J writes but if I could only claim one thing as MINE it would be that line. I cannot describe how special of a time that was for me.

Subsequently I think about fucking Minecraft of all things because of this one clip I have of me dealing with Bees in an old Hardcore world that is so engrained into my memory I can't help but think of it EVERY FUCKING TIME the song picks up here. If I can find the clip I'll have it embedded below but if not you'll just have to imagine me taking honey from a Bee hive without a Campfire to smoke them out, getting poisoned and flying away right as the song kicks back in with such precise timing you'd think I edited it in.


You have to believe me this isn't staged I was just very tired.

8/10

Track 2

bedroom community

The second track and the hot streak continues with one of my favourite glass beach tracks period. If you want to make me like a song you simply force the trans experience so far into my face I can't look away, this song makes me to look at it as if it's about me and I can't do anything to distract myself from how much I relate to Kate. (The character this song focuses on (Who is also the focus of yoshi's island in early demos)) and the spot on imagery of what it's like being in the trans community (most of us doing nothing to help ourselves).

I can't spend every Track review just quoting every line in every verse and that becomes so hard with this Song because I just want to paste them all in and say "This is sooooo me teehee (,,> ᴗ <,,)". I am in the form of a girl who hates her life, I am sitting alone on the floor of my bedroom and the one thing nobody can deny I do is wait for answers and waste time. The next verse, to me at least is a bit more "General trans" and doesn't hit me quite as specifically seeing as I thankfully didn't have much of a manly man childhood or upbringing. I played Tennis not Football they might as well have made my middle name faggot from the get go (I'd probably look cute in a Tennis skirt too...). But despite none of the Music I ever wrote being good or even published anywhere there is a little bit of a sting I feel from that next line "She writes emo songs, she's so depressed Her lyrics are naive but she still sings her heart out The only way she knows how to confess" Even if my old shitty breakup ballads did exist (and I'm very glad they no longer do) I think the line resonates with me more as the one SINGING, the emo songs, if not even a creative in a much more broad sense. I'm sure at least in some part me working on this website is the only way I know how to confess.

The next verse just has very solid imagery, one of those parts of a Song where you can perfectly imagine how an AMV with your OCs would look which to be fair I can do with half of J's catalogue but it felt worth it to point out here. Silly fun fact this line is why I started painting my Nails Black! I don't wear thigh highs nor have I made the commitment to dying my hair Black too (although it's been a strong consideration for a while so maybe one day). I also just really REALLY like the name drop of Mephistopheles, no aparent reason other than it makes the looming "Demonic" (Trans :3) force just a smidge more forboding which contrasts nicely with the idea of being in his arms, it has this double edged sword of fearful corruption of a pure Son from the perspective of the Traditional, Conservitive, American and almost an embrace of safety from the perspective of a Girl who finally has something or someone who understands her, and loves her for who she is.

I think this is the only time in the release version of the Album Kate gets name dropped with "He said you'll never be ok, 'kay," which I didn't even realise was in reference to the girl from the yoshi's island demos until I read what J wrote about kate said from the new album on it's blog, the entire time I thought it was more of a sort of stutter "You'll never be OK-K" but I think the songs benefit from the explicit mention of her as a character overall, it's something i'll probably talk more about in the yoshi's island section. I don't have much more to say about these 2 verses in particular but the last line "I thought he was wrong when he said that he loved me He's still thinking of me from up there" really just makes me think about my Dad. I don't want to assume who J is referring to here out of fear of treading too far into it's personal territory so I want to extra preface here that as with everything else in this review I'm coming at it from my own (probaby wrong most of the time perspective) but thinking about what my father promised me and then how I was treated after coming out, the promise of love no matter what only to pretend like I had never said anthing until I was left within and inch of my life over and over again hurts me so much. I really believed him every time he said those words and yet I'm left feeling like the lying manipulator because I can't believe him anymore.

The next verse decides to be a personally designed piece of psychological torment for me specifically because it's right after making me think about my daddy issues you want to bring up themes of suicide. I do love the double entendre of "Like it's not a murder, it's an inevitable tragedy" it's readable as a Suicide allogory obviously but also from the perspective of an outsider (which the best songs on this Album deal with a lot wink wink) the transition itself is a murder, the death of a young boy, of their son. While we're in talking about this verse I want to mention one of 2 segments I really miss from the demos of this track since a certain mashup of this song like to put it here, the line "I would rather go to hell then wake up tomorrow" from the bounce 01 demo. Not much to actually say about it other than I really like it and wish it found its way into the final version somehow :P

Wrapping up the Track is a reprise of the intro along with the other demo segment that I really miss which is the ending of the first demo with the final Song name (bedroom community demo 01.wav). I think this ending is significantly stronger of a closer to the Track, maybe you could argue that it's TOO finalistic for the end of Track number 2 but (how I interprit the muffled lyrics at least) just really hits that feeling of being "understood" and "saved" by someone, someone you LOVE being the one to help you not to transform you into something else but still as you, still in the form of a girl who hates her life. Is it cheating to say the unused demo verse is my favourite? Yes. Am I going to put it there anyway? Also yes. If you care that much otherwise the favourite verse pick is the nail polish one but argue with the wall <3

9/10

Another short one but this time with a little more meat to chew on, the opening "Ugh, I'm so miserable!" has always sounded more like "miiserable" to me so I like to imagine it's being said by an abandoned Wii balance board which actually kind of fits with the theme of deprication here. The line talking about wanting to help others but being to helpless to do any helping helps me relate to the themes of helplessness that help give the album it's identity. Very helpful. Track 5

neon glow

AND BACK TO IT oh my Godddddd!!! ☆(≧▽≦)☆ the flow here is just soso good even if i have no idea why the fuck we're using Aliens for symbolism now I can't care any less because the way each line bounces off of each other tickles my brain in such a pinpoint precise way with those synths. (I also don't know if the Aliens are meant to be representitive of anything it literally could just be Aliens here lmao).

Actually speaking on the lyrics now the idea of floating away mentioned brings things back to classic j so I think J really hates gravity and loves holding onto people (maybe more figuratively than literally). Looking at the annotations on Genius (which i fucking hate but I honestly wanted to get more meaning out of this song than Aliens) this Song takes a lot of inspiration from Juan Posadas who met the founder of Trotskyism after being kicked from the SOVIET UNION and the whole idea was to destroy modern civilization to rebuild it in a communist fashion that would attract Aliens. I have no clue how true of a connection that is but it is so insane that I actually don't think any jokes or analysis I write here could top it genuinely holy shit.

Track 6

cold weather

This song falls into the "Made specifically to pander to me" category but at least unlike all the other instances it's not because of trans related reasons it's because of long distance GAY reasons. I had never known what Mamegoma Lines was referring to whenever I listened to this song I honestly just assumed it was meant to be quotes from a book or musical for some reason but no it's talking about seal stickers for the messaging app line so I guess my equivilent is Sonic gifs over discord or something which is inherently a lot less cute.

Then we talk about missing the cold weather which falls back into the foggy streets of classic j which falls back into I MISS CANADIAN CHRISTMAS SO MUCH but it comparatively didn't take me 6 months to realise I knew even while it was happening to me in real time I would miss it more than anything in the world and the week leading up to returning to Australia wasjust as dreadfully painful to match.

Next chunk is just General relatability not so much lesbian long distance girlfriends but wrapping the verse up with "So I'll call in sick again just to spend the day with you" is very sweet and honestly brings me a little comfort with how much sometimes I prioritise working as much and making as much as possible, it's part of why I lost an ex of mine because every day I could be working feels like I'm screwing myself over to not but that leads to so much neglect you lose everything and everyone.

This song is very very good and I appreciate that it doesn't overstay its welcome like other Tracks can but still feels packed full unlike the others, just a very gay happy medium.

9/10
